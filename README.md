## About

I'm a Data Science student who spends most of my free time building things, not just studying them. My foundation is in Python, statistics, and classical ML — and over the last couple of years that's pulled me steadily toward NLP, retrieval systems, and LLM-based applications.

Alongside that, I work as a **Junior AI/ML Engineer at Alaide LLC**, where I get hands-on experience building production-oriented software — which keeps my AI/ML work grounded in systems that actually have to run, not just notebooks.

I care less about wiring together an API call and more about *why* a retrieval system returns what it returns, and whether an LLM's answer can actually be trusted. That's the thread connecting most of what I build.
